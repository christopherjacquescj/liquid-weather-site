FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Liquid Weather free?

Yes. The app is free, with no ads and no subscription.

Where does the data come from?

Forecasts come from Open-Meteo. The maps read open data directly from Météo-France (AROME, ARPEGE), DWD (ICON) and NOAA (GFS). The Moon is computed on the device; lightning comes from the EUMETSAT satellite; warnings from MeteoAlarm; wildfire risk from Copernicus EFFIS.

Are the maps radar?

No, and the app says so plainly: they are model forecasts, never radar observations. Only the lightning layer is a satellite observation.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Summaries and the AI assistant work on devices that support Apple Intelligence.

Do I have to enable location?

No. It is optional: you can pick a city by hand and change the permission any time in iOS Settings.

How do I contact you?
